Credits & scope

Segmentation by a single model of our own: an U-Mamba2 (Wu et al., MICCAI 2025 — first place in both ToothFairy3 tasks) finetuned on the ToothFairy3 challenge dataset (University of Modena and Reggio Emilia), trained here on 512 CBCT volumes and built on nnU-Net (Isensee et al., Nature Methods 2021).

The model inherits the ToothFairy3 dataset licence, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0: attribution, share-alike, and non-commercial use only.

Cases segmented before 2026-08-25 came from two published checkpoints we no longer run, both CC BY 4.0, and their downloads are still derived from those weights: DentalSegmentator (Dot et al.) and ToothSeg (van Nistelrooij, Krämer, Isensee et al.). Neither author endorses this service.

Research preview — not a medical device, and not for diagnostic use. CBCT grey values are not calibrated Hounsfield units, and these scans have no ground truth: every number in the quality panel is a claim about internal consistency, not about accuracy.
