1. What this service is

Dentistry CBCT automatically segments dental cone-beam CT and returns anatomical structures as RTSTRUCT, STL and NIfTI files, plus a browser viewer.

2. What it is not

It is a research preview. It is not a medical device, it has no regulatory clearance from any authority, and it is not for diagnostic use. Its output is a starting point for a qualified clinician, never a substitute for one. CBCT grey values are not calibrated Hounsfield units, so nothing it produces is a density measurement. You are responsible for reviewing every result before it informs any clinical decision.

3. Accuracy

We publish the measurements we can actually make and we are explicit about the ones we cannot — see what we do not claim. We make no warranty that a structure is correctly identified, correctly numbered, or complete. Quality findings are reported and never silently corrected.

4. Your responsibilities

  • You keep your sign-in credentials to yourself.

5. Plans and billing

A trial gives you 30 segmentations over 14 days, whichever runs out first, with no card. Paid plans are monthly, billed in advance through Stripe, and include a stated number of segmentations per calendar month. Unused segmentations do not roll over. One segmentation is one scan through the full pipeline; a re-run counts again; a failed job, or one you cancel before it reaches the GPU, does not count. Cancel at any time from your account page — access continues to the end of the paid period. We do not pro-rate partial months.

6. Availability

Segmentation runs on a single GPU, so jobs queue. We offer no uptime guarantee and may take the service down for maintenance. Results expire after 72 hours; download what you need.

7. The model, and what its licence permits

Segmentation uses a U-Mamba2 checkpoint we finetuned on the ToothFairy3 dataset. That dataset is released under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, and a model trained on it inherits the same terms: attribution, share-alike, and non-commercial use only. This service is a research preview and is offered on those terms. Upstream authors are credited on every page and do not endorse this service.
