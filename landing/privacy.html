Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 August 2026 · dentistry.dicomsegvr.com

This document is a plain-language template describing what the service actually does technically. It has not been reviewed by counsel and is not legal advice. If you process patient data under HIPAA, GDPR or LGPD, have your own adviser review it and put an appropriate agreement in place before uploading anything.

1. What we process

Two things: the imaging you upload (a dental CBCT as a DICOM zip or a NIfTI volume) and the account details your sign-in provides — an identifier, an email address and a username. Payment is handled entirely by Stripe; we store only a customer reference, never card details.

2. Imaging is not de-identified for you

A DICOM series carries patient identifiers in its headers. We read only the geometry we need and we do not index, search or share those headers — but we do not strip them either, and you should not assume a scan you upload has been anonymised. De-identify before uploading if your obligations require it.

3. How long we keep it

  • Your upload is deleted the moment a job completes. It is never read again, so there is no reason to keep it.
  • Results expire 72 hours after completion and are removed from disk. The download endpoint then answers 410 Gone.
  • The job record — timings, structure volumes and quality findings, with no imaging — is kept so your usage count and history remain accurate.
  • Curated example cases are public sample data and are exempt from expiry.

4. Separation between accounts

Every case is stored under its own account's prefix and every request is checked against the account that owns it. A request for another account's case is answered as if the case did not exist.

5. No trackers

No analytics, no advertising pixels, no third-party fonts or scripts. Every asset on this site is served from this domain. Sign-in uses our own identity service; your session token lives in your browser tab and is discarded when you close it.

6. Processors we use

  • Stripe — payments and subscription management.
  • Cloudflare — TLS termination and delivery of this site.

Segmentation runs on our own hardware. Your imaging is not sent to any third party and is not used to train anything.

7. Your rights

You may ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and everything under it. Deletion removes the account's stored cases in one operation. Write to support@dicomsegvr.com.

8. Where it runs

Processing and storage are in Brazil. If you are transferring personal data from another jurisdiction, satisfy yourself that you have a lawful basis for doing so.

9. Changes

Material changes will be announced on this page with a new date above. Continuing to use the service after that constitutes acceptance.
