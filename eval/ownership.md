# Structure ownership

Which model draws which structure, and why. Generated by `scripts/board_ownership.py`; a `TF3_BOARD` change is expected to cite it.

## The rule, committed to before these numbers existed

> A third-party model takes ownership of a structure only if it wins on `Dice·GT` **and** on detection error over fully-annotated cases, and is not contradicted externally. Wins under leakage do not count; losses do.

## Why most verdicts here are not `OWN`

TotalSegmentator trained on the public ToothFairy3 release and ToothSeg on ToothFairy2, a subset of it. This 20-case holdout is a split *we* made out of that same release, so both have almost certainly seen all 20 cases.

* A third-party **win** here is uninterpretable. A **loss** is real: leakage could only have helped.
* The detection leg cannot be measured. Only **3 of 20** cases annotate a complete dentition (ToothFairy3F_008, ToothFairy3F_041, ToothFairy3F_046) and all three are leaked.

One leak-free comparison exists, for ToothSeg only: 63 F + 417 P = 480 is exactly ToothFairy2's released training count, so the 52 `S` cases are the ToothFairy3 addition. **Verify that against ToothFairy2's published case list before any verdict rests on it.**

## Verdict

**No third-party model takes ownership of any structure.** The board ships our own canal specialist and nothing else in `apply` mode; both foreign models are mountable in `shadow` mode, where they run, record what they would have drawn, and stamp nothing.
